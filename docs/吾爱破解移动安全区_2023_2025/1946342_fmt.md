## 教程开始
1. 提取安装包并打开 `dex` 文件，方法名搜索 `isvip`，找到第二个方法名进去
![](https://attach.52pojie.cn/forum/202407/22/114601wgykyfgmbzdzbovo.jpg)
2. 选择 319 行的代码转成正则表达式
![](https://attach.52pojie.cn/forum/202407/22/114707plmiu4aizvlie51a.jpg)
![](https://attach.52pojie.cn/forum/202407/22/114710clcm16omla889xl6.jpg)
3. 转换完成后回到管理器并且搜索此正则表达式，然后替换为 `const/4 $1, 0×2`
![](https://attach.52pojie.cn/forum/202407/22/114847hshedssxhzqijj5s.jpg)
![](https://attach.52pojie.cn/forum/202407/22/114850mp3iqbppulzgxqgc.jpg)
4. 接下来解锁云端上传功能，常量分别过滤“鉴权失败”和“数据正在”，分别删除掉方法名内的所有判断语句（如下图所示）
![](https://attach.52pojie.cn/forum/202407/22/115155yhvt9ll0mi9ccvu9.jpg)
![](https://attach.52pojie.cn/forum/202407/22/115158junl5lq77oqn4n55.jpg)
![](https://attach.52pojie.cn/forum/202407/22/115201t2dcyqzc5dncdfa7.jpg)
![](https://attach.52pojie.cn/forum/202407/22/115204il71s1kl4l8364jl.jpg)
![](https://attach.52pojie.cn/forum/202407/22/115207nx68npooppobxqpp.jpg)
5. 最后保存去签安装即可
![](https://static.52pojie.cn/static/image/hrline/5.gif)
